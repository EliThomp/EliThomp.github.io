 <h1>TalkStory Proposal Idea</h1>

  <h2>Overview</h2>
  <p><strong>List of Proposers:</strong> Gordon Chun, Elias Thompson, Ethan Ibanez, Shaylon Mizukami</p>

  <h2>The Problem</h2>
  <p>Over a third of all students at UH come from out of state, and adjusting to life on a new island can be challenging. Visitors and newcomers often struggle to find practical, everyday solutions to local problems—like where to park near popular beaches, how to avoid traffic bottlenecks, or where to get affordable, ono (delicious) food. The lack of a centralized, community-driven resource makes it difficult to access this kind of insider knowledge.</p>
  <p>At the same time, many local residents and students offer small-scale services—such as haircuts, handcrafted jewelry, or photography—but have limited means to promote themselves to a wider audience. This creates a disconnect between those seeking help and those offering it. Our project aims to bridge that gap by building a platform where users can both share local tips and advertise their gigs, creating a space for connection, support, and community resource-sharing.</p>

  <h2>The Solution</h2>
  <p>Our proposed solution is to create a community-driven website that serves as a digital hub for local tips and gig promotion. The platform will feature a forum-style layout where users can easily share and discover advice on navigating island life—whether it’s the best times to find parking in busy areas, hidden beach spots, or cheap eats around campus.</p>
  <p>Additionally, it will offer a dedicated section for individuals to advertise their small-scale services such as tutoring, haircuts, photography, or other side gigs. By combining local knowledge and self-promotion opportunities, the website welcomes both newcomers and longtime residents to connect and support each other.</p>

  <h2>Mockup Page Ideas</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Home Page</strong>: Intro to website's functionality and purpose, navigation links, most-liked tips and gigs.</li>
    <li><strong>Login / Signup Page</strong>: User authentication system.</li>
    <li><strong>Forum Page</strong>: Thread-style layout with categories like Parking Hacks, Cheap Eats, Hidden Spots, and Campus Tips. Includes upvotes, user stamps, and post dates.</li>
    <li><strong>Gigs & Services Page</strong>: Grid layout similar to Fiverr. Posts have tags, title, description, and contact info.</li>
    <li><strong>Profile Page</strong>: Displays user information and allows editing of previous posts.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Use Case</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Out-of-State Student cannot find parking at UH Manoa and uses the Parking Hacks forum to find good areas to park.</li>
    <li>Local Barber decides to promote their side gig and creates a service post.</li>
    <li>A user shares a photo and description of a great meal in the Cheap Eats forum.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Beyond the Basics</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Reputation System</strong>: Users who frequently post helpful tips gain higher reputations.</li>
    <li><strong>Gamification</strong>: Users earn badges or titles for hitting milestones (e.g., number of posts).</li>
    <li><strong>AI-Powered Search or Recommendations</strong>: Smart suggestions based on browsing behavior and keywords.</li>
  </ul>
